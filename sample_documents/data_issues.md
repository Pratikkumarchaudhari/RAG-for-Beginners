# Data issue management procedure
Fictional learning material. Not an employer policy.

A data issue record includes an issue ID, affected dataset, description, business impact, priority, Data Owner, assigned Data Steward, target date, current status, and supporting evidence. The Data Steward investigates and tracks remediation. The Data Owner approves the business rule and confirms resolution.

Critical issues that block a scheduled report are escalated to the Data Owner on the same business day. Other unresolved issues are discussed in the monthly governance meeting. Close an issue only after repeating the failed validation and documenting the corrected result. Retain the original evidence for traceability.
