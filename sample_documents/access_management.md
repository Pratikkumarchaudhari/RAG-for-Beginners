# Access management procedure
Fictional learning material. Not an employer policy.

A user requests dataset access by submitting their name, business purpose, requested role, and requested duration. The Data Owner approves or rejects the request. The administrator implements the approved role assignment. The Data Steward verifies that the access record is complete. Requesters may not approve their own access.

Grant access through roles using the principle of least privilege. Review access quarterly. Remove access when the business need ends or the user leaves the team. Record the approver, approval date, assigned role, and removal date in the access register.
